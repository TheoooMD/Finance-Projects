# Finance-Projects

Interactive finance and FP&A projects by **Minh-Duc Theo HO**.

## Projects

### [FP&A Terminal](fpa-terminal/) — *FP&A Financial Modeling, Valuation & Capital Allocation Engine*

A single-file, browser-based FP&A terminal: an integrated three-statement model
with a debt schedule, a DCF and reverse-DCF valuation stack (tornado sensitivity
and Monte Carlo included), budget-vs-actual variance and PVM bridges, and a
capital allocation engine covering ROIC vs. hurdle, economic profit and covenant
headroom.

**▶ Live demo:** https://theooomd.github.io/Finance-Projects/fpa-terminal/
· [Documentation](fpa-terminal/README.md)

---

Everything here runs client-side — no backend, no install, no data leaves the
browser.
