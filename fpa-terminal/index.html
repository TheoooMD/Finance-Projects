DEMO FIELD GUIDE

How to evaluate this demo

Use the fictional demo company to inspect the workflow and challenge its assumptions.

ILLUSTRATIVE DECISION CASEFP&A → ACTION

Can the demo company keep investing in growth while protecting liquidity?

QUESTION

Should leadership continue planned hiring and operating spend, or stage investment until margins and cash conversion are clearer?

ANALYSIS

Trace margin and working-capital movements, update the hiring and operating plan, compare base and downside forecasts, then review cash headroom, covenants and incremental returns.

DECISION RULE

Release investment in stages only when downside liquidity remains above minimum cash and expected returns clear the selected hurdle. Otherwise defer lower-priority roles or define a funding action.

This demonstrates a decision framework. The conclusion must follow the model outputs and is not a claim about the synthetic demo results.

01 · DATA

Know what you are looking at

The FY2023–FY2025 financial statements and operating ledger through August 2026 are synthetic showcase data. No live company filings or market feed are used.

02 · EXPLORE

Trace a question through the model

Start with a performance or working-capital movement, inspect its drivers, then change a forecast scenario. Follow the impact through cash, covenant headroom, ROIC and valuation, and finish with the board narrative.

03 · INTERPRET

Challenge the assumptions

Outputs depend on the loaded statements and editable inputs. Risk probabilities are conditional on the displayed independent ranges; valuation inputs are illustrative and should be verified. Treat the results as analytical examples, not forecasts or investment advice.

Suggested path: Profitability → Variance → Plan → Model → Risk → Capital Allocation → Board Pack
