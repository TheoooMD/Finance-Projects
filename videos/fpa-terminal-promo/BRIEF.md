## Intent

A promo for FP&A Terminal — a single-file HTML terminal that takes a company's
raw financial statements and returns the whole FP&A loop: an integrated
three-statement model, variance bridges, covenant and liquidity stress tests, a
DCF, and a board-ready CFO memo.

The audience is Senior FP&A and corporate finance people who already know how
long this work takes by hand. The video is shared on LinkedIn to impress them
and make the time saved obvious. Tone: Bloomberg-terminal professional, dense
and credible, never a bouncy startup promo. Visually appealing through precision
and rhythm, not decoration.

Built by Minh-Duc Théo HO; the video doubles as a portfolio piece.

## Customizations

- Feature the site's own captured screens as the video's assets — this is a
  showcase, not an invented interpretation. Every number on screen is real
  output from the terminal's demo company (Halcyon Cloud Systems, synthetic).
- The five-pillar spine DATA → ANALYZE → PLAN → DECIDE → COMMUNICATE is the
  video's structure, because it is the product's structure.
- Feature the `<GO>` command line: senior finance people recognize it instantly
  and it signals "built for us, not for a generic SaaS buyer".
- Count-up on the headline time figure in the hook.
- End on the CFO memo's red flags — the single most expensive deliverable the
  terminal replaces.

## Notes

- **Silent by design.** The project is not signed in to HeyGen and the local TTS
  and music engines have missing deps, so there is no narration and no BGM. That
  suits the destination: LinkedIn feed plays muted, so on-screen kinetic
  typography carries the whole message. The user can add a music track in
  LinkedIn, or we can add one later if they sign in or supply a file.
- Halcyon Cloud Systems is a fictional sample company. Keep the "Sample data"
  chip legible wherever a screen shows its numbers — do not imply these are a
  real company's filings or a live market feed.
- Never claim the tool replaces judgment. The promise is time and coverage, not
  a decision made for the analyst.
- No stock photography, no generic SaaS gradients, no bouncy easing. Amber on
  near-black, mono type, hard precise moves.
